Hallween Party 2025

The event was held on November 1st, 2025, in New York City. To prepare, our entire team spent about a month and a half planning, including deciding on the venue, developing social media concepts, planning our budgets, testing out the ticket purchasing system etc. I was also responsible for securing event performances, which required me to contact various artists and DJs in New York and directly addressed any of their concerns related to necessary equipments and the soundcheck. It was considered my part-time job during that month, which made balancing the workload alongside my full-time one quite tough. Ultimately, the event was a great success and I gained so many valuable skills and created wonderful memories along the way.

I remember managing social media posts was the most challenging task due to the heavy workload. Since everyone had busy schedules, finding time for team meetings to brainstorm designs and content was difficult. As being the lead, besides overseeing the design process and giving feedback to my team, I also had to conduct quality assurance on the content before posting to make sure it was fun and engaging and the content itself still remains appropriate.
